Strategic Decision Takeaways for Emily Johnson

1. SUV Dominance
SUVs represent over 44.8% of global revenue and deliver the highest consumer willingness-to-pay. OEMs must prioritize next-gen midsize SUV platforms.
2. LFP Cost Defensibility
LFP adoption has crossed 52% of total passenger deliveries, slashing cell pack costs below $85/kWh and unlocking mass-market price parity with ICE vehicles.
3. Range Satisfaction Threshold
Customer ratings plateau once real-world driving range crosses 460 km. Beyond this, fast-charging speed and infotainment UX drive satisfaction more than pack size.

Strategic Advisory for Automotive Consulting Clients

Duopoly Dynamics
BYD and Tesla command over 46% combined global delivery volume. Emerging entrants must avoid head-on sedan battles and target commercial fleets and 2-wheelers.
China Supply Chain Moat
China produces 58% of global EV units, leveraging localized gigafactory ecosystems. Regional tariffs make domestic assembly in North America and EU essential.
Value Quadrant Leadership
Vehicles priced between $28,000–$38,000 with 4.5+ customer ratings achieve the steepest sales velocity and lowest dealer inventory holding days.
